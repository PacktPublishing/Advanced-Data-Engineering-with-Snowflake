Write a dbt model named gold_customer_segments that classifies customers from
gold_customer_value into four segments based on lifetime_revenue:
- 'platinum' for revenue >= 50000
- 'gold' for revenue between 10000 and 49999
- 'silver' for revenue between 1000 and 9999
- 'bronze' for revenue below 1000
Materialize as a materialized view. Include a segment_assigned_at timestamp column.
Reference gold_customer_value via ref().
